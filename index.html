Before You Join

Is the ebook included with Academy?

Yes. Academy members get the Strategy Ebook included. Non-Academy members can buy standalone access for £20.

Where do members log in?

Members log in with Whop. Once signed in, the portal checks the member's Whop access and opens the right Academy, Discord, Ebook, or Free area.

Can course videos be watched on the site?

Yes. The member area is designed for embedded course lessons. We can use YouTube unlisted embeds short-term, then upgrade to Vimeo, Bunny, or VdoCipher later.
